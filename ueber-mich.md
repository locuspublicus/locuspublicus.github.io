Hello there, I am Hakbard.

*I created this alter ego based on a novel I read as a teenager. Back then I thought: "This is too fucked up, man." Today I believe it was just a tiny reflection of how strange the society we humans have built really is.

Let me be honest: you won't agree with me on many of the topics I write about, and that's okay. Disagreement isn't the enemy. Indifference is.

I believe we are all longing for the same thing: a good life, a purposeful life, a life in which we are free in the most basic sense. And my freedom ends where yours begins.

To get there, every human being needs a few fundamentals: a decent shelter, enough water and food, transportation, access to technology, the chance to move their body, and healthcare. Only when these basics are covered can we start to overcome the ego and the violence, and begin to understand life in all its natural weirdness.

Instead, we mistake freedom for luxury, success for status, and enlightenment for a perfectly curated feed. We chase more, when what we actually need is enough.

This blog is my attempt to explore that gap between the life we're sold and the life we're longing for. Sometimes angry, sometimes curious, always honest.

Welcome to Locus Publicus, a public place. Pull up a chair. You don't have to agree. You just have to think.*
