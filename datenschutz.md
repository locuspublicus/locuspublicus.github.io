## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you (Art. 15)
- have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16)
- have your data deleted (Art. 17)
- restrict how your data is processed (Art. 18)
- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
- lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work (Art. 77)

To use any of these rights, send an email to the address above.

## 7. Visitors from outside the EU

This policy follows the GDPR, one of the strictest privacy standards worldwide, and applies to all visitors wherever they are. This website does not sell or share personal information within the meaning of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar laws.
